The disposable server lifecycle runner and tests are adapted from Cha0sCollective's [Ch4oS-MVT](https://github.com/Cha0sCollective/Ch4oS-MVT/tree/7ce3ef44910aeb5d95fc799badfcd73e450d4171), commit `7ce3ef44910aeb5d95fc799badfcd73e450d4171`.
